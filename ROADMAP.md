# Roadmap

TRV Road Tile Editor is led by its author and lead developer, **Mayrik PADEY**, who decides the direction
of the project and what gets merged (the "benevolent dictator" model used by many community projects).

**The goal: a board editor** for Thunder Road: Vendetta and Ignition, possibly with new board shapes for the
games' expansions. It is **not meant to become an all-purpose app**: no dice roller, no virtual tabletop.
This page tells you what is planned, so you can check whether your idea fits before proposing it
(see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)). The detailed working plan, in French, is in
[docs/roadmap.md](docs/roadmap.md).

## Released

- **1.3.1**: barricades layered from left to right along a border (the right one over the left one).
- **1.3**: barricades (Ignition), placed on the borders between rows with a "Barricade" tool, on both board
  formats; confirmations and messages shown in the page (fixes the sound lost in Safari after "Clear board").
- **1.2**: free decorations (place, move, remove), contextual tips under the palette.
- **1.1**: brush painting, undo / redo, keyboard shortcuts, automatic saving, project files, US paper
  sizes and PDF export, print-scale ruler, faster loading, new look, sound effects, credits page.

## Next

- Nothing scheduled for now: ideas are welcome (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## Maybe later

- Export of a board to the format of a digital adaptation of the game (long term, optional).
- Board without title band, with a 7th row of spaces (requested by players; not planned for now).
- **Ignition curved roads**: wanted, waiting for accurate measurements of an original curved board (a flat,
  top-down photo with a ruler would help a lot).
- **Ignition Rubble terrain**: someday, artwork resources needed.

## Out of scope

These will not be accepted:

- Anything commercial (ads, paid features, selling exports or code).
- Features outside board editing: dice rollers, virtual tabletops, game rules engines, etc.
- Extracting the games' artwork from this project to use or distribute it elsewhere, or adding new
  artwork reproduced from the games without Restoration Games' permission.
